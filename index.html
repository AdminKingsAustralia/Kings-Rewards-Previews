What to review

In the complete demo, use Show signup popup to open the invitation anytime. Use sample details to try signup and the personalised thank-you animation.

Can you understand your next reward? Are the benefits and order minimums clear? Try 450, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 points. Redeem, simulate a purchase, complete a delivery, then refund it.

The live plugin requires staging acceptance before production. This review does not prove payment, email or Square POS integration.

Where we are now

The customer experience is ready for team review. Plugin code for signup, welcome discounts, delivery entitlements, points, redemption and refund adjustments has been built for staging tests. The next step is to install it on a test WooCommerce site and check the full journey before launch.

Email/SMS delivery and Square POS loyalty synchronisation are not connected yet. Referral, birthday, member pricing and VIP features are later phases.

A couple of details while watching the video: the first reward is $10 off at 500 points, not 10% off. Account access and reward redemption will require verified identity; simply typing someone’s email will not give access to their rewards.
